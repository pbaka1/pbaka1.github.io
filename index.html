PAUL BAKA

BIO

I am a medical doctor trained in Ghana and currently pursuing my Master of Public Health (MPH) at Johns Hopkins University. My long-term ambition is to become a pediatric oncologist, dedicated to improving the lives of children battling cancer. As part of my MPH journey, I have taken advanced coursework in statistical computing, with a focus on building strong programming and data analysis skills using R. I believe these skills are essential for modern medical research, especially in the age of big data and precision medicine. My research interests center on novel genetic sequencing approaches to address pediatric hematologic cancers. I am deeply motivated to explore innovative strategies that can translate cutting-edge science into practical, lifesaving therapies for children.
